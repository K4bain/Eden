# Ask Matt

## Main Flow: Idea → Ship

1. **grill-with-docs** — sharpen the idea by interview (start here with a codebase)
2. **Branch — can you settle every question in conversation?**
   - No → **prototype** → **handoff** back
3. **Branch — multi-session build?**
   - Yes → **to-spec** → **to-tickets** → **implement** per ticket
   - No → **implement** right here

## On-Ramps

- **Bugs piling up** → **triage**
- **Something's broken** → **diagnosing-bugs**
- **Huge foggy effort** → **wayfinder**

## Codebase Health
